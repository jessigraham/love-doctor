# Love Doctor

A self-discovery tool built on adult attachment theory. It walks you through what kind of relationship you're looking for, your attachment style (or a quick check to find it), who pairs best with you, and how to grow toward secure attachment.

## What's in it

The tools below the journey open from the profile or the "Skip to all tools" link.

- **Guided journey**: the main experience, one screen at a time. It covers what you're looking for, your attachment style (pick it or take a 12-statement check), your current relationship if you have one (pick their style or describe them), who's a good match for you, and how to grow toward secure. It ends in a personal profile with self-awareness notes, what a good match looks like, and next steps.
- **Compatibility meter**: score, typical moment, strengths and friction for every pairing of secure, anxious, avoidant and disorganized
- **Relationship check-in**: an honest read on whether a current relationship is likely to work
- **What you're looking for**: goal alignment between you and a partner
- **Find your style**: a 12-statement self-check scored on anxiety and avoidance
- **Read your partner**: pick traits and describe how they act to get a best guess at their style
- **Grow toward secure**: a path for each style, plus guidance on choosing a better match

## Running it

It's a single `index.html` with no build step. Open it in a browser.

The "deeper read from Claude" button only appears when the page runs as a Claude artifact. Everywhere else the partner reader uses its built-in quick read.

## Note

This is a tool for self-reflection, not a diagnosis. Scores are general tendencies drawn from attachment research.
